How Air Conditioning Built the Sunbelt

Air conditioning began as a humidity solution for industrial printing, then transformed theaters, offices, homes, architecture, migration, energy use, and electoral power. This episode follows mechanical cooling from Willis Carrier's early systems to the mass-produced window unit and the postwar growth of the Sun Belt, while keeping the environmental bill in view.
